Scripts & frameworks
LMPAMA seller qualification + key talking points for Monmouth County
LMPAMA seller framework
L
Location
"Can you tell me the address of the property?"
M
Motivation
"What's making you consider selling right now?" Listen hard — this is everything. People sell for reasons, not prices.
P
Price
"Do you have a number in mind?" Don't anchor first if you can avoid it. Let them go first.
A
Asking condition
"How would you describe the condition? Any updates or repairs needed in the last 10 years?"
M
Mortgage
"Is there a mortgage on the property? Roughly how much is owed?" You need to know if there's equity to work with.
A
Appointment
"I'd love to come take a look — are you free [day] or [day]? Takes 20 minutes."
Cold call opener
"Hi, is this the owner of [address]? My name is Christian, I'm a local real estate investor in Monmouth County. I noticed your property and wanted to reach out — are you at all open to a cash offer on the house? I'm not an agent, no fees, no commissions, close on your timeline."
"What's your offer?" — deflect & qualify
"I'd love to give you a fair number — I just want to make sure I'm not wasting your time with a number that doesn't work. Can I ask a few quick questions about the property first? Takes two minutes."
"I need full price" — reframe
"I completely understand. Our offers are typically below retail — but the trade-off is no repairs, no showings, no contingencies, close in 2–3 weeks. Is speed or simplicity at all important to you in this sale?"
